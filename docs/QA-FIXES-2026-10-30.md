# BeyondBinary QA improvements — 30 October 2026

This records changes made after the original [QA report](QA-2026-10-30.md).
The existing page layout, classifiers, three-claim limit and warmed GPU runtime
are retained. This is a correction and traceability pass, not a claim of perfect
fact-checking or calibrated political analysis.

## Decision and influence definitions

Evidence eligibility uses **heuristics**, not learned or calibrated trust scores:

- MiniLM sentence similarity must reach 0.45.
- A named subject, when detected, must overlap the passage.
- The audited URL and duplicate selected text/URLs cannot independently corroborate
  themselves. Different domains alone do not prove editorial independence.
- All returned NLI vectors remain visible, including excluded evidence. An inference
  failure produces no invented probabilities.

Eligible entailment or contradiction at least 0.72 is decisive. Both kinds present
means conflicting evidence and an unresolved verdict. Otherwise the decisive side
determines the verdict, with the mean winning NLI score shown as confidence. Neither
that score nor the dashboard number is a calibrated probability of factual accuracy.

Influence removes one passage from the same scored retrieval set and recomputes the
verdict. It also reports the change in mean `(entailment - contradiction)` over
eligible passages. A large margin effect need not change the final verdict. Ties
are possible, and the UI says when the verdict remains unchanged.

## Remaining limits and next work

1. Relevance thresholds, source checks and verdict confidence still need evaluation
   on a labelled dataset. They reduce demonstrated failures; they do not establish
   general accuracy or verify every source's authority/independence.
2. Political BERT can assign partisan signals to nonpolitical text and react to tone.
   The comparison now discloses its actual meaning and text inputs, but model/domain
   calibration and tone-invariance are not solved by relabelling the output.
3. The polarization formula still measures stance probabilities and the blended
   publisher/text spectrum. It is not a factual-overlap metric.
4. Automatic discovery of another same-event article is not implemented. The wire's
   now-working manual route requires a second real URL when no alternate is supplied.
5. A blocked publisher may yield only an exact indexed excerpt or an extraction
   error; pasted text remains the reliable alternative. Browser automation cannot
   safely be treated as unrestricted public URL fetching.
6. Print-dialog execution, cross-browser/device coverage, a clean Render/Docker
   redeployment, sustained load and exhaustive provider outages remain outside
   this tested pass. This laptop/public tunnel is the verified runtime.
